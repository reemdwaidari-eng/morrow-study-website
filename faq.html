THE LITTLE DETAILS

A few things
you might wonder.

Helpful notes about Morrow's digital products.

What is a digital product?

A digital product is a file you can access electronically, such as a PDF planner or printable tracker. No physical item is shipped.

How do I use a printable planner?

Download the PDF after purchase (once a real checkout is connected), then print it at home or through a print shop. Check the product page for its page size and format.

Can I use the planner on a tablet?

The first Student Planner edition is designed as an A4 PDF for printing. A tablet-friendly version may be offered separately in the future.

Can I share or resell the files?

Products are intended for personal use only unless a product's license says otherwise. Please don't redistribute, resell or claim the designs as your own.

Are purchases available right now?

This is an early storefront prototype. Payments, order processing and automatic file delivery have not been connected yet.

How can I ask another question?

Visit the Contact page. The contact form is currently a visual demo and needs a secure form service before launch.
